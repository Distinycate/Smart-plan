## Unit boundary
Core target = **finished past events with Past Simple**. Present Perfect is not required.

## Can-do outcomes
Describe a short completed event; ask/answer past questions; use affirmative/negative Past Simple; sequence events; add a reaction; relay key facts.

# Lesson 1 — A finished event
Yesterday I visited my aunt. We had lunch at her house. After lunch, we watched a film. I went home at six o’clock.

Regular: visit→visited, watch→watched, start→started  
Irregular: go→went, have→had, see→saw, buy→bought, eat→ate, take→took, meet→met

Use Past Simple with closed finished time: yesterday, last Saturday, two days ago.

# Lesson 2 — Negative past
`subject + didn’t + base verb`

I didn’t go out. / We didn’t take a taxi. / She didn’t buy anything.  
❌ I didn’t went. → ✅ I didn’t go.

Practice: I didn’t ___ the film. → **see** · They didn’t ___ late. → **arrive** · “She didn’t bought food.” → **She didn’t buy food.**

# Lesson 3 — Questions
Where did you go? / Who did you go with? / What did you do? / What time did you arrive? / Did you like it?

Rule: `did` carries past marking; main verb returns to base form.

# Lesson 4 — Sequence and reaction
Sequencers: first, then, after that, later, finally  
Reaction: It was fun / interesting / tiring. I was surprised. I really enjoyed it.

### Reading `TXT-A2-03-L04-001` — A rainy day in Khon Kaen
Last Sunday, Mali went to Khon Kaen with two friends. First, they visited a small market near the station. Mali bought a bag and her friends bought snacks. Then they walked to a café because it started to rain. They stayed there for about an hour and talked about their school days. After that, the rain stopped, so they went to a park. They took some photos, but they didn’t stay long because the ground was very wet. Finally, they took the bus home at six. Mali was tired, but she really enjoyed the day.

Questions: Where? **Khon Kaen** · After market? **rain/café** · Stay long at park? **No** · Why? **wet ground** · Final feeling? **tired but positive**

# Lesson 5 — Listen and relay
### Listening `AUD-A2-03-L05-001`
Last Saturday, Pim went to a night market with her sister. They arrived at seven. First, they looked at clothes and small gifts. Pim bought a T-shirt, but her sister didn’t buy anything. Then they ate noodles and had fruit juice. At about nine, it started to rain heavily. They didn’t have an umbrella, so they took a taxi home. Pim was tired, but she enjoyed the evening because she found a nice shirt and spent time with her sister.

Targets: companion **sister** · purchase **T-shirt** · sister **didn’t buy anything** · taxi reason **rain/no umbrella** · reaction **tired but enjoyed it**

## Listening where a negative changes the answer `AUD-A2-03-V04-002`
> On Sunday, Arun planned to visit a museum with his cousin. They arrived at eleven, but the museum was closed for a special event. They **didn’t go inside**. Instead, they walked to a riverside market. Arun **didn’t buy any clothes**, but he bought lunch for both of them. They stayed until four and then took a bus home.

Questions:
1. Did they visit the museum exhibits? → **No**
2. Did Arun buy clothes? → **No**
3. What did he buy? → **lunch**
4. Where did they spend most of the day? → **riverside market**

## Second reading `TXT-A2-03-V04-002`
Two weeks ago, Nida joined a one-day cooking class. She arrived early because she didn’t know the area well. First, the group prepared vegetables and learned how to make a simple curry. Nida forgot one ingredient, so her partner shared some with her. After lunch, they made a dessert. Nida didn’t like the dessert very much, but she enjoyed the class because the teacher was friendly and the instructions were easy to follow. She went home with two recipes and tried one again the next day.

Tasks: identify sequence, one negative fact, one problem/solution and Nida’s mixed reaction.
