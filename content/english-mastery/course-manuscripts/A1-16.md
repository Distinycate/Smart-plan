Reading A
Our class has a welcome day on Saturday. It starts at nine at the community centre. I am on the welcome team. I ask visitors their first names and give them name cards. The reading room is on the left. The café is next to it. A drink costs ten baht. At ten, we meet in the main room for a game. Visitors can ask us for help.

Read for purpose, then locate the people, events, conditions and limits. Read one short sentence at a time. Find names, numbers, places and times. Point to the words that answer a question. Ask for repetition instead of guessing.

Reading B

## Listen, clarify and interact

Listening script B
Thank you for coming. Our game starts at ten. Please go to the main room. Bring your name card. After the game, you can read or visit the café. The welcome team can help you find the rooms.

First pass: identify purpose and main message. Second pass: note essential details and any corrections. Check against the script only after the listening attempt when approved audio becomes available. For now, practise with a teacher or partner reading the script.

Pronunciation and prosody
Keep the final sound of names and key words audible. Pause between ideas. Say numbers slowly and check them with a partner; intelligibility matters more than imitating an accent.

Interaction rehearsal: take both roles. Ask one clarification question, answer it using the source, and check that the other person has understood. Do not equate quietness, accent or hesitation with lack of ability.

Planning sequence
1. Identify audience and purpose.
2. Select source details that the audience needs.
3. Mark any uncertainty, unconfirmed arrangement or missing information.
4. Choose two language features from Lesson 2.
5. Produce the response, then check meaning before surface accuracy.

Mediation: explain the essential information to a Thai-speaking classmate who has not read the sources. Preserve dates, quantities, conditions, agency and uncertainty. Say what cannot be concluded.

Online interaction: respond to a request for clarification, then add a follow-up checking question. Acknowledge corrections and revise your message where needed.
