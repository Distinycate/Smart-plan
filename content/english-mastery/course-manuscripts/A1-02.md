# A1-02 — Greetings & Introductions

Version 0.6.0 · PRE_HUMAN_CONTENT_COMPLETE · Preserved pilot derivative; human review pending.

## เป้าหมาย
1. greet and respond in a familiar situation;
2. introduce name, country/origin and a simple role;
3. ask and answer very simple personal questions;
4. spell a name;
5. use one repair phrase when communication breaks down;
6. write a 3–5 line online introduction;
7. relay two simple facts about another person.

## Hello. I’m…

**Model**
A: Hello. I’m Mina. What’s your name?  
B: Hi, Mina. I’m Ton. Nice to meet you.  
A: Nice to meet you too.

**Key language:** Hello / Hi / I’m… / My name is… / What’s your name? / Nice to meet you.

**Thai note:** `I’m Ton.` หรือ `My name is Ton.` ใช้แนะนำชื่อ ไม่ใช้ `I name Ton.`

### Grammar: be in introductions
| Subject | be | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am / ’m | I’m Ton. |
| you | are / ’re | You’re Mina. |
| he | is / ’s | He’s Narin. |
| she | is / ’s | She’s Lina. |

## Sorry, how do you spell that?

### Listening `AUD-A1-02-L03-001`
A: What’s your name?  
B: Narin.  
A: Sorry. Please say that again.  
B: Narin.  
A: How do you spell that?  
B: N-A-R-I-N.  
A: Thank you.


**Repair phrases:** Sorry, I don’t understand. / Please say that again. / How do you spell that?

## Language models
- be: I am / I'm: identify/self-introduce
  I’m Mali.
- be: you are / you're: identify interlocutor
  You’re Ken.
- be: he/she is: relay simple identity
  She’s a nurse.
- Where are you from?: ask origin
  Where are you from?
- repair questions: request repetition/spelling
  Please say that again.
