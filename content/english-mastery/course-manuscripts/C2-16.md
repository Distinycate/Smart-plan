## Understand the situation

Integrated C2 Performance
เป้าหมาย: จัดทำผลงานบูรณาการที่รักษาความขัดแย้ง หลักฐาน และการเปลี่ยนสารตามผู้รับ

Use the language of integrated c2 performance to complete the unit's practical or interpretive task.
Identify and relay the essential information in the two readings without adding unsupported facts.
Respond to the two listening scripts with appropriate clarification and source-sensitive wording.

Reading A
A fictional consortium must decide whether to expand an open learning service. The funder prioritises visible reach, educators question whether attendance captures learning, and community partners warn that a digital-only expansion could exclude some intended users. The sources contain both shared aims and incompatible assumptions about success. An integrated response must reconstruct these assumptions, compare feasible options and recommend a course of action with explicit decision conditions. It should also translate the recommendation for different audiences without presenting unresolved disagreement as consensus. Sophisticated performance is demonstrated through accurate selection, justified qualification and responsive interaction, not through density of vocabulary or an appearance of effortless certainty.

Read for purpose, then locate the people, events, conditions and limits. Reconstruct the logic and rhetorical purpose of competing accounts. Test presuppositions, compression losses and shifts of agency. Preserve principled disagreement while producing a precise, audience-sensitive synthesis. Consider more than one defensible interpretation and explain why you prefer one.

Reading B
Briefing pack: Funder—renewal requires an agreed evaluation plan, not a specific attendance target. Educators—learning evidence must include more than visits. Partners—retain a supported access route during any trial. Operations—current staffing supports two additional sessions weekly. No expansion budget or permanent timetable has been approved.

Compare the two inputs: what is shared, what is additional, and what is not known? Use only details actually supplied. All characters, organisations, figures and events in these inputs are fictional classroom materials.

## Listen, clarify and interact

Listening script A — authored script; recorded audio and human audio QC are pending. Reading the transcript is text-based preparation, not evidence of independent listening ability.

A: Can we announce agreement to expand?
B: We have agreement to examine a trial, not authorisation for permanent expansion.
A: What should the recommendation include?
B: Two feasible options, the evidence each would generate, the access condition and the decision still required from the board.

Listening script B
Give a concise oral briefing, respond to a sceptical question and revise one point when a partner identifies a genuine omission. Then prepare a public explanation and an internal decision record. Preserve the same facts and conditions across genres, and make any change of interpretation visible rather than quietly rewriting the evidence.

First pass: identify purpose and main message. Second pass: note essential details and any corrections. Check against the script only after the listening attempt when approved audio becomes available. For now, practise with a teacher or partner reading the script.

Pronunciation and prosody
Use prosody strategically to manage complex interaction, signal parenthetical qualification and distinguish reported from personal stance. Judge communicative effect and intelligibility, not resemblance to an idealised native accent.

Interaction rehearsal: take both roles. Ask one clarification question, answer it using the source, and check that the other person has understood. Do not equate quietness, accent or hesitation with lack of ability.
