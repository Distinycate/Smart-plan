## Understand the situation

Critical Academic Synthesis Writing
เป้าหมาย: เขียนสังเคราะห์เชิงวิชาการที่แยกความต่างทางแนวคิดและระดับหลักฐาน

Use the language of critical academic synthesis writing to complete the unit's practical or interpretive task.
Identify and relay the essential information in the two readings without adding unsupported facts.
Respond to the two listening scripts with appropriate clarification and source-sensitive wording.

Reading A
Two fictional studies use the term engagement differently. One counts contributions during seminars; the other analyses reflective journals. A review that reports one positive and one negative finding without discussing this conceptual divergence creates a misleading dispute. Critical synthesis should explain what each operationalisation makes visible and what it leaves unmeasured. It can then ask whether the studies address competing accounts of the same phenomenon or different dimensions of a broader construct. The writer's contribution lies partly in this reconstruction, but an elegant conceptual framework must remain answerable to the sources. A proposed explanation should be marked as an interpretation, not retrospectively attributed to the original researchers.

Read for purpose, then locate the people, events, conditions and limits. Reconstruct the logic and rhetorical purpose of competing accounts. Test presuppositions, compression losses and shifts of agency. Preserve principled disagreement while producing a precise, audience-sensitive synthesis. Consider more than one defensible interpretation and explain why you prefer one.

Reading B
Study notes: Study A—more spoken contributions after a new discussion format; no measure of contribution quality. Study B—richer reflective journals among some quieter participants; no comparable baseline. Both studies concern one class and neither tests long-term learning. No significance tests are supplied in this exercise.

Compare the two inputs: what is shared, what is additional, and what is not known? Use only details actually supplied. All characters, organisations, figures and events in these inputs are fictional classroom materials.

## Listen, clarify and interact

Listening script A — authored script; recorded audio and human audio QC are pending. Reading the transcript is text-based preparation, not evidence of independent listening ability.

A: Can I conclude that discussion format improves engagement?
B: Specify the dimension and the limits. A counts contributions; B examines reflection without a comparable baseline.
A: Where does my own synthesis enter?
B: In explaining the relationship between those measures, clearly labelled as your interpretation.

Listening script B
Organise the review around a conceptual question rather than a procession of article summaries. Use attribution to distinguish reported findings, methodological criticism and your own reconstruction. End with a question the evidence leaves open, specifying what kind of observation could help resolve it without claiming a research design guarantees certainty.

First pass: identify purpose and main message. Second pass: note essential details and any corrections. Check against the script only after the listening attempt when approved audio becomes available. For now, practise with a teacher or partner reading the script.

Pronunciation and prosody
Use prosody strategically to manage complex interaction, signal parenthetical qualification and distinguish reported from personal stance. Judge communicative effect and intelligibility, not resemblance to an idealised native accent.

Interaction rehearsal: take both roles. Ask one clarification question, answer it using the source, and check that the other person has understood. Do not equate quietness, accent or hesitation with lack of ability.
