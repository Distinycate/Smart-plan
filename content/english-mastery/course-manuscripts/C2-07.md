## Understand the situation

Rhetoric & Persuasive Strategy
เป้าหมาย: ประเมินกลวิธีโน้มน้าวควบคู่กับคุณภาพเหตุผลและทางเลือกที่ถูกละ

Use the language of rhetoric & persuasive strategy to complete the unit's practical or interpretive task.
Identify and relay the essential information in the two readings without adding unsupported facts.
Respond to the two listening scripts with appropriate clarification and source-sensitive wording.

Reading A
A campaign for a fictional cultural centre frames the choice as either embracing the future or retreating into irrelevance. The antithesis is memorable, but it excludes intermediate options such as a limited trial or a partnership. Repetition of the phrase our shared future invites identification with the proposal before its costs are examined. Neither device makes the proposal wrong; rhetorical effectiveness and evidential adequacy are separate questions. A critical response should describe the persuasive mechanism, reconstruct the substantive case and restore excluded alternatives. Simply naming a fallacy can become another shortcut if it replaces analysis of the actual reasons and consequences at issue.

Read for purpose, then locate the people, events, conditions and limits. Reconstruct the logic and rhetorical purpose of competing accounts. Test presuppositions, compression losses and shifts of agency. Preserve principled disagreement while producing a precise, audience-sensitive synthesis. Consider more than one defensible interpretation and explain why you prefer one.

Reading B
Budget note: The full centre would require a three-year commitment. A smaller partnership could be reviewed after six months. The campaign speech mentions the first option's potential reach but does not compare its reversibility with the partnership. No decision has yet been authorised.

Compare the two inputs: what is shared, what is additional, and what is not known? Use only details actually supplied. All characters, organisations, figures and events in these inputs are fictional classroom materials.

## Listen, clarify and interact

Listening script A — authored script; recorded audio and human audio QC are pending. Reading the transcript is text-based preparation, not evidence of independent listening ability.

A: The speech persuaded the room. Does that settle the case?
B: It shows persuasive success with that audience, not necessarily a sound comparison.
A: What should our response do?
B: Acknowledge the shared aim, then compare reach, cost and reversibility across the available options.

Listening script B
Track where the audience is invited to identify, fear, hope or infer. Then ask which invitation is supported by a reason and which relies on an unexamined association. Effective criticism can retain the speaker's legitimate aspiration while rejecting the false restriction on how it might be pursued.

First pass: identify purpose and main message. Second pass: note essential details and any corrections. Check against the script only after the listening attempt when approved audio becomes available. For now, practise with a teacher or partner reading the script.

Pronunciation and prosody
Use prosody strategically to manage complex interaction, signal parenthetical qualification and distinguish reported from personal stance. Judge communicative effect and intelligibility, not resemblance to an idealised native accent.

Interaction rehearsal: take both roles. Ask one clarification question, answer it using the source, and check that the other person has understood. Do not equate quietness, accent or hesitation with lack of ability.
